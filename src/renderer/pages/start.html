Clearnet links open through Tor like any other site. Onion links are full addresses — hover or copy them from the address bar.

How it works here

  • Tor is launched automatically and your browser talks only to its local SOCKS5 proxy.
  • Each tab gets its own session and its own Tor circuits (circuit isolation).
  • WebRTC is disabled and DNS never leaves the Tor process.
  • No history, cookies, or cache are written to disk between runs.
