System requirements

PlatformMinimum
WindowsWindows 10 or 11, 64-bit
macOSmacOS 11 (Big Sur) or newer, Intel or Apple Silicon
Linux64-bit distribution with FUSE (AppImage); a glibc-based desktop is recommended
Disk~350 MB free (app + bundled Tor)
NetworkOutbound connections to Tor relays (TCP 443/9001 and others)
On networks that block Tor: the app may never reach 100% bootstrap. Add bridges via a torrc fragment in the app's tor-data folder — see troubleshooting.
