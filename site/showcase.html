Designed around the way Tor works

Per-tab circuits

Each tab is its own session with unique SOCKS credentials, so two tabs never share a circuit. Closing a tab discards everything it held in memory.

One omnibox

The address bar accepts clearnet URLs, search queries and .onion names in the same input. A scheme badge and a lock make the current context obvious.

Transparent status

The Tor chip reports live bootstrap progress and expands into a full diagnostic log — no black boxes.
